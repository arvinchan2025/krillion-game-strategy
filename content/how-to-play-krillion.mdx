Krillion is an online trivia game built around open-ended prompts and answer discovery. Instead of choosing from a fixed list of multiple-choice options, players think of answers that fit a prompt.

This guide is an independent introduction. The live game is hosted at [krillion.io](https://krillion.io), and its interface and rules may change. Always follow the instructions displayed in the game itself.

## 1. Open the official game

Visit [Krillion](https://krillion.io) to see the current game experience. The official homepage currently links to Daily Dive, Unlimited, Puffer Party, Archive, Themed Packs, My Krillion, Friends, Settings and FAQ.

## 2. Read the prompt precisely

Before typing an answer, identify the category and any restrictions in the wording. A prompt asking for an animal, place, object or person may have a different accepted-answer set and different aliases.

Do not assume that a related answer is automatically valid. Check whether it satisfies every part of the prompt.

## 3. Think in more than one category

Start with familiar examples, then broaden your search. Useful thinking directions include:

- Common examples that immediately come to mind.
- Less familiar species, places, names or specialist terms.
- Regional and historical terminology.
- Alternative names and accepted aliases.

The point is to generate thoughtful candidates, not to enter random obscure words.

## 4. Learn from the official explanation

The creator’s [FAQ](https://krillion.io/faq) says answer sets are built by aggregating and cleaning relevant datasets such as dictionaries, wiki pages and encyclopedias. The source material depends on the question.

The FAQ also explains that some prompts can use familiarity data for mechanical rarity scoring, while other prompts need LLM assistance and manual review.

## 5. Explore the available modes

The official homepage currently links to Daily Dive, Unlimited, Puffer Party, Archive and Themed Packs. This guide has separate [mode notes](/modes) for the details that can be verified.

## A note about scoring

Do not treat this guide as an answer key or a predictor of the game's internal scoring. The official FAQ says the top answer is not selected by rarity alone: fun factor can also matter.

## Where to play

Play the original game at [Krillion.io](https://krillion.io). This website does not host, reproduce or embed the game.
