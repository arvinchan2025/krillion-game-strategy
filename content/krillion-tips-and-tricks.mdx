The most useful way to improve at open-ended trivia is to build a consistent thinking process. These tips are general strategies, not a promise about Krillion's internal ranking.

## Read the exact wording

A prompt's restrictions matter. Do not answer a broader or easier question than the one being asked.

## Move past the first thought

List the obvious answers, then deliberately switch to another subject area. This reduces the chance that you submit only the most familiar examples.

## Think in names and aliases

The official FAQ says answer sets can include aliases. When researching a subject, look for alternate names and common synonyms, but make sure the candidate still refers to the same thing.

## Prefer a defensible answer

If you cannot explain why an answer fits, it may be a weak candidate. Relevance is more important than choosing a word solely because it sounds obscure.

## Learn from feedback

If the game accepts an unexpected answer, consider whether an alias or alternate spelling explains it. If a reasonable answer is rejected, the creator's FAQ acknowledges that source coverage can sometimes miss an answer.

## Keep expectations realistic

The creator says the Krillion pick considers fun factor as well as rarity. There is no public formula that guarantees the rarest or highest-scoring answer.
