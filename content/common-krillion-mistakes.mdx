## Mistake 1: Ignoring the wording

An answer can be interesting but still fail to meet the prompt. Check every restriction before submitting.

## Mistake 2: Choosing obscurity over relevance

A rare-sounding answer is not useful if it does not fit. The creator's FAQ also says the Krillion pick considers fun factor rather than rarity alone.

## Mistake 3: Thinking every subject has one name

The official FAQ explains that accepted answers can have aliases. Consider alternate names, but verify that they refer to the same subject.

## Mistake 4: Assuming the accepted list is perfect

The creator acknowledges that sources can be missed. A plausible answer may be absent from the curated set.

## Mistake 5: Treating community guesses as official scoring rules

Player theories can be useful for discussion, but they should not be presented as confirmed mechanics unless the creator or live game documents them.
